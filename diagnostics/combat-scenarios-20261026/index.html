Player confirmation check

Ten real combat confirmations advanced 10 turns. Target HP lost: 0. Loaded rifle rounds before/after: 8/8. This agrees with the current handler: targeting text, event log and a turn advance, without actor damage.

What must exist before win-rate tests are meaningful

The runtime needs NPC enemy selection and engagement, an authoritative actor attack/damage/death path, weapon/ammunition handling for those attacks, and explicit combat skill inputs. Structural damage already has a separate authority; its weapon-force values were not repurposed into invented actor damage. Until these paths are connected, longer tournaments cannot establish weapon or skill balance.
