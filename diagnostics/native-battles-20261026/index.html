The Mechanist - Native battle recordings

The game's rendered views, artwork, effects and positional sound. Four seeded fights, each captured in normal tile view and Doom view.

These videos come directly from GamePanel rendering. No diagram substitutes. Sound uses the packaged WAV assets through the same stereo mixer and room reflections as live gameplay.
Open video

Press Play and use the player's volume control for sound. Battles advance one real game turn every half-second in the recording. Normal lighting, directional visibility and occlusion remain active. The isolated observer has Vision 42, using the existing capped range of 14; normal tile zoom is 100%.

What changed

NPC attacks now trigger actual weapon cues and shared particle effects. Blocked firing lanes route around occupants rather than waiting indefinitely. Fresh Doom viewports respect the observer's facing. Sandbag and barricade bindings now point to visually checked defense artwork, replacing the unrelated hardware selected by a legacy asset ID.
